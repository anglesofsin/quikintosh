# Minimal weather artwork

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool, November 25, 2026. Each PNG is a cropped and resized cell of the generated sheet; no programmatic illustration was used.

Prompt: A transparent 4 × 2 sheet of eight minimal flat weather interface glyphs, centered in equal cells with padding. Reading order: sun, partly cloudy, overcast, rain, heavy rain, thunderstorm, snow, crescent moon. Smooth off-white clouds, a subtle gray second cloud, muted yellow sun and lightning, blue precipitation. No dimensional rendering, shadows, glossy reflections, texture, atmospheric background, text, borders or panels.

These assets replace the previous detailed artwork. The weather UI draws no procedural drops, lightning flashes or atmospheric overlays.
